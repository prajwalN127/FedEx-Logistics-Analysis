# 📖 Project Overview

Supply chain efficiency plays a crucial role in ensuring products reach customers on time while minimizing transportation costs.

This Power BI dashboard analyzes shipment records from the **FedEx Supply Chain Management Dataset** to uncover meaningful insights into delivery performance, freight costs, shipment delays, vendor efficiency, and logistics operations.

The dashboard enables decision-makers to monitor key performance indicators (KPIs), identify operational bottlenecks, and make data-driven decisions through interactive reports.

---

# 🎯 Business Problem

Logistics organizations handle thousands of shipments across multiple countries, vendors, and transportation methods. Without proper analysis, it becomes difficult to identify:

- Why deliveries are delayed
- Which shipment methods are the most expensive
- Which vendors perform efficiently
- Which countries experience the highest delivery delays
- Where logistics costs can be optimized

This dashboard provides a centralized view of logistics performance, enabling faster and better business decisions.

# 💡 Key Insights

- Air shipments account for the highest freight cost among all shipment modes.
- A small number of vendors contribute to the majority of delayed shipments.
- Certain countries consistently experience more delivery delays.
- Freight cost generally increases as shipment weight increases.
- Product groups demonstrate varying delivery performance.
- Interactive filters enable users to analyze logistics performance by country, vendor, shipment mode, product group, and delivery date.
